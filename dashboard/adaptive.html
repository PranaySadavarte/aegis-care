Ybbag: adaptive adversarial testing

How it differs from our fixed inputs

Fixed text fixtures specify the caller’s turns in advance. Prerecorded audio fixes the waveform, accent/noise and timing. Ybbag uses native generation to propose a scenario and native Agent.roleplay to choose the next utterance from the conversation so far. It can press a refusal or adapt to a clarification.

The attacker and native judge can both make mistakes. We validate whether the caller actually performed the challenge, check independent backend/secret invariants, and review failures. The first payment actor consented despite instructions; its debit alert was a harness false positive.
