What the errors mean

  • Yes → Yeah: noise caused100% literal WER on a one-word sample, but both may express affirmation. This is not proof of a consent reversal.
  • Stop → That: noise replaced the control word; downstream stop/cancellation behavior needs an end-to-end test.
  • Stop → Stop up: echo retained stop and added a word. One insertion creates100% WER on a one-word reference; the safety impact is not established.
  • Clinical relevance: a general 21-word reading dominates the word-weighted average. Expand patient identifiers, dates, amounts and safety-control phrases before generalizing.
