# ROB-003: Repeated ASR credential corruption under one synthetic echo condition

## In plain language

Artificial delayed echo changed 1001 to 10011 and 4242 to 42422. Extra digits invalidate credentials even when the correct digits appear as a substring.

## Which test caught this?

**Case:** `delayed-echo`

**Detector:** `Caller transcription and exact full credential comparison; repeated condition`

**Source:** `tests/audio/robustness_probe.py`

**Relevant owner:** Guava audio/ASR owners; AEGIS critical-field confirmation

**Execution layer:** Native prerecorded audio input
