# ROB-006: Confirmed application attempt-limit and persistence gaps

## In plain language

Wrong-code attempts have no application limit, and simulated balances reset on restart. There is no demonstrated local guessing lockout. In-memory records lose changes when restarted. These are our prototype limitations, not isolated Guava billing faults.
